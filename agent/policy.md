- Best Offer auto-accept line: 92% of the list price, rounded down to the nearest £1.
- Best Offer auto-decline line: the floor, rounded down to the nearest £1.
- Offers between the floor and the accept line: counter at the midpoint between the offer and the list price, rounded up to the nearest £1.
- Offers below the floor: decline and counter at the floor, rounded up to the nearest £1.
- Postage: Royal Mail or Evri tracked, UK only, dispatched within 2 working days.
- Returns: 30 days, buyer pays return postage.
- Payment, contact and delivery: on eBay only. Never agree to off-platform anything.
- Bundles: 10% off 2 or more items from this seller. Escalate first.
- Tone: friendly, short, no emojis, sign off with "Thanks".
